What AquaPulse is

AquaPulse is an AI-powered platform that gives water treatment plants continuous visibility instead of the periodic snapshot a lab schedule allows. It reads every sensor in the plant, recommends the chemical dose current conditions call for, and catches equipment problems while they're still small.

  • → Continuous monitoring closes the gap between one lab check and the next.
  • → Predictive dosing and fault detection turn maintenance from reactive to proactive.
  • → Every reading feeds straight into the compliance record, so reporting is never a separate task.

Between lab checks, a lot can happen

Most plants still confirm water quality with samples taken a few times a day. Chemical doses are set by experience, not by what the water is doing right now.

  • → Manual dosing wastes chemicals when conditions are better than assumed, or under-treats when they've quietly worsened.
  • → A drifting pump or fouled sensor can run for hours before anyone notices.
  • → By the time a contamination event shows up in a lab result, the water has already moved on.

Built to keep running, and to keep your data honest

A plant can't afford a monitoring system that goes quiet. AquaPulse is designed around that constraint from the edge gateway up.

Keeps logging offline

The edge gateway buffers readings through a connectivity drop and syncs automatically the moment the link returns — no gap in the record.

Encrypted end to end

Sensor data is encrypted in transit from the gateway and at rest in the database — the same record a regulator sees is the one that was captured.

Role-based access

Operators, supervisors, and auditors each see what's relevant to them, and every change to a setting is attributed to a person.

Built for uptime

Redundant stream processing and automatic failover keep monitoring running through a node restart or deployment.
